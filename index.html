I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, working with Dr. Lauren Gillespie in the GAIEA Lab and Dr. JJ Park in the Park Lab. I'm broadly interested in machine learning for Earth and ecological systems across scales, from individual plants and local extremes to global fields, and especially in the fine-scale structure and rare events that current models tend to smooth away. My work spans geospatial foundation models, field reconstruction from sparse observations, and physics-grounded modeling of extremes, though my previous work has also spanned multimodal learning for scientific data, satellite monitoring of marine ecosystems, and neuroimaging.

Before Michigan, I got my B.S. in Computer Science at UCLA and did research with NASA JPL and Brookhaven National Laboratory. I'm supported by the DOE CSGF, GEM, and Rackham Merit fellowships.

Research

  • OmniField: Conditioned Neural Fields for Robust Multimodal Spatiotemporal Learning

    Kevin Valencia, Thilina Balasooriya, Xihaier Luo, Shinjae Yoo, David Keetae Park

    ICLR 2026 · paper · code

    A neural field that fuses sparse, irregular measurements from several modalities to reconstruct and forecast spatiotemporal fields, and stays robust under heavy sensor noise.

  • Uncertainty-Calibrated Spatiotemporal Field Diffusion with Sparse Supervision

    Kevin Valencia, Xihaier Luo, Shinjae Yoo, David Keetae Park

    Preprint 2026 · paper

    A diffusion model trained directly on sparse observations that reconstructs and forecasts full fields with calibrated uncertainty.

  • Detection of Coral Bleaching using PlanetScope Imagery using the CT Classification Model

    Mariam Ayad, Kevin Valencia, Christine Lee, Bjorn Lütjens, Raphael Kudela

    Under review, International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation

    Reef-scale bleaching detection from PlanetScope SuperDove imagery, evaluated on reefs held out from training. Done at NASA JPL.
