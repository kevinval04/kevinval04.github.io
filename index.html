About

I teach models to reason about how natural systems move through space and time — from satellites overhead to ecosystems on the ground.

Starting Fall 2026, I'm a PhD student in EECS at the University of Michigan, joining the GAIEA Lab (advised by Dr. Gilles Pl) — a new group at the intersection of AI, ecology, and environmental science. My work spans neural fields, spatiotemporal diffusion models, and multimodal learning, applied to remote sensing, ecological monitoring, climate and weather, and wildfire risk.

I earned my BS in Computer Science at UCLA (2026), with research affiliations at NASA JPL and Brookhaven National Laboratory.

Research

Selected research

I connect modern generative and representation learning to the physical structure of Earth systems — so models can forecast, reconstruct, and reason about a changing planet.

OmniField: Conditioned Neural Fields for Robust Multimodal Spatiotemporal Learning

Kevin Valencia, Thilina Balasooriya, Xihaier Luo, Shinjae Yoo, David Keetae Park · Preprint 2025

A continuity-aware conditioned neural field that fuses sparse, irregular, multimodal measurements — via a multimodal crosstalk block and iterative cross-modal refinement — for reconstruction, forecasting, and cross-modal prediction without gridding, and stays robust under heavy sensor noise.

arXivCode

Reef-Scale Coral Bleaching Detection from Satellite Imagery

Kevin Valencia, et al. · NASA JPL · 2026

A satellite pipeline that detects coral bleaching from PlanetScope SuperDove imagery, validated with leave-one-site-out evaluation to test real-world generalization across reefs.

PaperCode

NeuroX-Field: Zero-Shot Neural Field Diffusion for EEG Super-Resolution

Co-author · Kevin Valencia · Preprint 2026

Zero-shot diffusion in neural-field space that spatially super-resolves EEG across electrode layouts, without retraining for each montage.
