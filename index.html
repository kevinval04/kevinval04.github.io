About

I teach models to reason about how natural systems move through space and time — from satellites overhead to ecosystems on the ground.

Starting Fall 2026, I'm a PhD student in EECS at the University of Michigan, joining the GAIEA Lab and Park Lab (advised by Dr. Lauren Gillespie and Dr. JJ Park) a new group at the intersection of AI, ecology, and environmental science. My work spans neural fields, spatiotemporal diffusion models, and multimodal learning, applied to remote sensing, ecological monitoring, climate and weather, and wildfire risk.

I earned my BS in Computer Science at UCLA (2026), with research affiliations at NASA JPL and Brookhaven National Laboratory.

Research

Selected research

I connect modern generative and representation learning to the physical structure of Earth systems so models can forecast, reconstruct, and reason about a changing planet.

OmniField: Conditioned Neural Fields for Robust Multimodal Spatiotemporal Learning

Kevin Valencia, Thilina Balasooriya, Xihaier Luo, Shinjae Yoo, David Keetae Park Â· Preprint 2025

A continuity-aware conditioned neural field that fuses sparse, irregular, multimodal measurements using iterative cross-modal refinement for reconstruction, forecasting, and cross-modal prediction without gridding, and stays robust under heavy sensor noise.

Reef-Scale Coral Bleaching Detection from Satellite Imagery

Mariam Ayad, Kevin Valencia, Christine Lee, Bjorn Lütjens, Raphael Kudela · NASA JPL · 2026

Developed a reef-scale satellite intelligence pipeline for automated coral bleaching detection from PlanetScope SuperDove imagery, combining geospatial feature extraction, site-held-out evaluation, and cross-reef generalization analysis to support scalable monitoring of reef health.

Uncertainty-Calibrated Spatiotemporal Field Diffusion with Sparse Supervision

Kevin Valencia, Xihaier Luo, Shinjae Yoo, David Keetae Park · Preprint 2026

SOLID, a mask-conditioned diffusion framework that learns directly from sparse observations, reconstructing and forecasting full spatiotemporal fields while producing calibrated uncertainty estimates.
